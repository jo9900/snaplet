# Privacy policy

Effective date: September 15, 2026

Snaplet is a local screenshot and annotation app maintained by [jo9900](https://github.com/jo9900).

- **Screen content:** Snaplet captures screen content only when you request a screenshot. The selected image and annotations remain in memory while you edit. It does not upload screenshots.
- **Saved images:** Images are written only to the location you choose, or copied to the macOS clipboard at your request. Other applications can access clipboard content according to macOS behavior. Locations synchronized by iCloud or another service follow that service's settings.
- **Preferences:** Application preferences are stored locally. Launch at login is managed by macOS and can be disabled in Snaplet Settings or System Settings.
- **Data collection:** Snaplet has no analytics, tracking, advertising, accounts, network services, or third-party SDKs. The developer does not collect data through the app.
- **Permissions:** Screen capture permission is used for screenshots. The file save dialog grants access to your chosen destination. You can revoke screen capture permission in System Settings.

To remove images, delete the files you saved. To replace clipboard contents, copy something else. Removing the app does not remove screenshots you saved outside it.

For privacy questions, [open an issue](https://github.com/jo9900/snaplet/issues) without including personal information or private screenshots.
